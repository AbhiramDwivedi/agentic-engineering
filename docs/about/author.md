# About the Author

*Who wrote this reference, what experience it draws on, and where to find me elsewhere.*

I'm Abhiram Dwivedi. Most people call me Ram. I wrote this reference and the companion repo.
It is a personal, open-source project.

I've spent 22 years in software engineering and architecture, at two employers: Infosys from
2004 to 2010, and Publicis Sapient since 2010, where I'm a senior architect.

## Relevant experience

- **A multi-agent estimation platform** (Publicis Sapient, internal, 2025 to present). It turns
  RFP documents into staffed, priced proposals. I'm its architect; I led the team that built it
  and wrote more than 80% of the code.
- **A digital web platform for a Fortune 500 hospitality company** (2022 to 2026). I led a
  platform engineering organization of about 40 people for a web platform serving more than
  15,000 transactions per second.
- **DevOps at Fannie Mae Multifamily** (2016 to 2020). I started out writing Java microservices
  and went on to own the DevOps roadmap. Deployment times fell by 85%.
- **Earlier:** a mortgage securitization platform and investment-bank systems at Publicis
  Sapient, and, at Infosys, an onsite stint at Apple in Cupertino from 2007 to 2008.
